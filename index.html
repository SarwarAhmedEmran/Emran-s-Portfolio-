About

Currently in my fourth year of undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Engineering at Metropolitan University Bangladesh, I am dedicated to both academic achievement and active campus leadership. I have completed major courses including Structured Programming (SP), Data Structures (DS), Algorithm, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Database Management System (DBMS), Compiler Construction, Computer Organization and Architecture (COA), and Computer Engineering (CE), along with programming languages such as C, C++, Java, Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I have contributed to multiple academic projects through teamwork and collaborative initiatives.

My leadership experience includes roles as Secretary of the CSE Society, Secretary Program at the Metropolitan University Rover Scout Group, Rover Met in the Metropolitan University Rover Scout Group, former President of MUKCYF, Organising Secretary at the Knocking Collaborators Youth Foundation, Publication Secretary at the Metropolitan University Research Society, and Commandant at the Metropolitan University Disaster Response Unit. Deeply passionate about learning and research, I am actively involved with the Centre for Strategy and Policy Innovation (CSPI) and the university’s research society, striving to explore new ideas, organize events, and contribute to innovative, impactful solutions in my field.

Alongside my academic and leadership pursuits, I am a professional photographer and photo editor with extensive experience in wedding and event photography. I specialize in capturing meaningful moments and delivering high-quality, visually compelling images through expert color grading and retouching. My work reflects creativity, attention to detail, and a commitment to excellence, which I continuously develop through hands-on projects and client engagements.
